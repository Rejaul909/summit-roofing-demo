07 FAQ

Common
questions.

Don't see your question? Call us, or add it to your estimate request and we'll answer it.

Ask a question
How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes take one to three working days. Larger or steeper roofs, complex details and weather can add time. Your written estimate includes a planned schedule.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The inspection and written estimate are free, with no obligation to book. You keep the photos either way.

Do you help with insurance claims after a storm?

We document damage with photos and measurements and can meet your adjuster on site. Coverage decisions are always made by your insurance company, not by us.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

It depends on the roof's age, how widespread the damage is, and the condition of the decking underneath. After the inspection we'll lay out both options and the trade-offs, so you can decide.

What roofing materials do you install?

Architectural asphalt shingles, standing-seam and exposed-fastener metal, and concrete or clay tile, plus seamless aluminum gutters.

Do I need to be home during the work?

No. We'll ask for access to an outdoor outlet and the driveway. Your project lead will send updates and photos at the end of each day.
